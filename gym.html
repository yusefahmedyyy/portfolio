Train anywhere.
No gym required.

YourGym is a mobile workout app built for flexibility and user autonomy. Create custom routines matched to your goals, fitness level, and whatever equipment you have on hand, then take them offline and train wherever you are.

Core Features

01

Custom Workout Plans

Build routines from an exercise library sorted by muscle group, difficulty, and duration.

02

Video Guidance

Every exercise includes clear instructions and video demos to keep your form correct and safe.

03

Progress Tracking

Logs every workout, tracks personal bests, and visualizes progress through simple charts.

04

Offline Mode

Plans and videos stay available with no connection, so you can train anywhere, anytime.

05

Adaptive Workouts

Suggests changes in difficulty based on your feedback and workout history.

06

Minimalist Design

A clean interface built for quick access and distraction free training sessions.
