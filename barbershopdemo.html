Client App

What the client taps

A digital chair card that replaces the paper punch card, built for a client booking their next fade in under a minute.

  • ✓
    Digital loyalty card
    Live points balance and tier status, always in the client's pocket.
  • ✓
    One tap booking
    Rebook the same barber and the same cut in a couple of taps.
  • ✓
    Reward redemption at checkout
    Points apply straight to the bill, no punch cards to carry.
  • ✓
    Appointment reminders
    Push alerts before a visit and before points are about to expire.
  • ✓
    Referral tracking
    Bonus points land automatically when a referred friend books a first cut.
  • ✓
    Visit history and style notes
    Every past cut saved, so a new barber can pick up where the last one left off.
Shop Dashboard

What the owner sees

A single console for running the chairs, the schedule, and the loyalty program without a spreadsheet in sight.

  • ✓
    Live appointment calendar
    Every chair, every barber, updated the moment a client books or cancels.
  • ✓
    Loyalty program builder
    Set point rules, tiers, and the reward catalog without touching code.
  • ✓
    Automated win back campaigns
    A message goes out on its own once a regular has been away too long.
  • ✓
    Client profiles
    Visit history, preferences, and lifetime spend in one place per client.
  • ✓
    Revenue and redemption reporting
    Daily numbers on chair revenue and points redeemed, exportable for payroll.
  • ✓
    Barber performance tracking
    Bookings, tips, and commission per barber, side by side.
